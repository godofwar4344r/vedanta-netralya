Our heritage

A legacy of ocular excellence.

From a single trusted clinic into the Kumaun region's elite super-specialty eye institute — built on one promise: ethical, world-class care.

Founded in 2003, Vedanta Netralya has grown by investing where it matters: robotic laser systems, high-definition diagnostics, and dedicated micro-surgical wings — all under the leadership of Dr. R.J.K. Singh, Dr. Sameer Varma, and Dr. Kanhaiya Mittal.

A newly built, fully air-conditioned hospital designed so that precision medicine never feels clinical — only calm.
