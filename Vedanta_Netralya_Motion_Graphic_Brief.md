- **Background Music:** Deep, ambient electronic track with a slow, steady pulse (minimalist and cinematic).
- **Sound Effects (SFX):**
  - Scene 1: Soft "shimmer" or "whoosh" during particle convergence.
  - Scene 2: High-tech "digital hum" or "laser pulse" sound.
  - Scene 4: Soft "ping" or "ding" as the logo settles.

## 5. Technical Specifications for Designers
- **Resolution:** 1920x1080 (HD) or 1080x1920 (Vertical for Reels).
- **Frame Rate:** 30fps or 60fps for maximum smoothness.
- **Lighting:** Global illumination with strong rim lighting on metallic elements.
- **Render Engine:** Octane, Redshift, or Cycles for the premium glass/metal look.
